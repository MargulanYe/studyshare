# StudyShare

A free, open-source web platform where students find, share and review study materials.

## Project goals and SDGs
StudyShare supports **UN SDG 4 (Quality Education)**, targets 4.5 and 4.c, by giving anyone free access to learning resources. It also contributes to **SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities)** by lowering the financial barrier to study materials.

## Features
- Upload and browse study materials by subject
- Rate and comment on materials
- Report inappropriate content to moderators
- Export your data in JSON/CSV
